# Support Policy for QvReader

We want your experience with QvReader to be smooth and productive. This document explains the available support channels and scopes.

---

## 1. Community Support

For general questions, discussions, troubleshooting, and usage tips:

- **GitHub Discussions**: [Ask Questions & Share Ideas](https://github.com/qvcloud/QvReader/discussions)
- **GitHub Issues**: [Report Bugs or Request Features](https://github.com/qvcloud/QvReader/issues)
- **Documentation**: [docs/](docs/) directory contains usage guides, shortcuts, architecture notes, and features.

Please search existing issues and discussions before opening a new one.

---

## 2. Supported Platforms

We actively support and test QvReader on:

- **macOS**: 11 (Big Sur) and newer (Apple Silicon & Intel x86_64)
- **Windows**: Windows 10 (1809+) and Windows 11 (x86_64)
- **Linux**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS+, Fedora 38+, Arch Linux (x86_64)

---

## 3. Scope of Support

### In Scope
- Core desktop client bugs (rendering, editor, shortcuts, encoding, file export).
- Performance regressions or memory leaks.
- Build/compilation issues on supported platforms with standard toolchains.

### Out of Scope
- Custom third-party forks or unofficial patched builds (please consult the fork's maintainers).
- Commercial license queries, invoice inquiries, or paid account issues (see Commercial Support below).
- Security vulnerabilities (please use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

---

## 4. Commercial & Official Distribution Support

If you have purchased an official Pro license or have questions regarding payment, invoices, or official notarized builds:

- **Contact**: `support@qvreader.com`
- **Official Website**: `https://qvreader.com`
- Note: Never post license keys, transaction IDs, or payment tokens in public GitHub issues or discussions.
