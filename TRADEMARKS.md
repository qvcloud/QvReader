# Trademark Policy

This Trademark Policy outlines the permissible use of the **QvReader** name, logo, and brand assets.

While the QvReader source code is licensed under the permissive [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE), Section 6 of Apache-2.0 explicitly excludes trademark rights. This policy defines how our trademarks may be used.

---

## 1. Permissible Uses (Descriptive Use)

You **MAY**:
- Use the word "QvReader" in text to truthfully describe your software's compatibility, interoperability, or origins (e.g., *"This extension is compatible with QvReader"*, or *"This application is a fork of QvReader"*).
- Distribute unmodified official binary packages obtained directly from official GitHub Releases (`qvcloud/QvReader`) under their original names.
- Mention QvReader in articles, blog posts, reviews, and tutorials.

---

## 2. Requirements for Unofficial and Community Builds

If you compile QvReader from source, modify the code, or create a third-party distribution:

- **Identifier Requirement**: You **MUST** identify your build as an unofficial or community build. Specifically, you must display `"Community Build"` or `"非官方构建"` in the application's window title and About dialog.
- **Logo and Branding**: You may retain the base SVG icons for non-confusing community builds under Apache-2.0, but you **MUST NOT** state or imply that your build is an official release produced or endorsed by the QvReader project.
- **Digital Signatures**: Official digital signatures (Apple Developer ID notarization, Windows Authenticode signing) belong exclusively to official maintainer distribution channels. You must not claim official signature status.

## 3. Prohibited Uses

You **MAY NOT**:
- Use "QvReader" or confusingly similar variations as the primary name of a commercial product, service, company, or domain name without written authorization.
- Use official QvReader brand marks or logos in a manner that falsely suggests sponsorship, partnership, or endorsement.
- Distribute modified versions of QvReader under the unadorned official name without the required Community Build disclaimer.
